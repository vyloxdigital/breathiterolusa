Breathiterol is a 60ml / 2 fl oz liquid dietary supplement taken orally. Unlike traditional capsule-based supplements, Breathiterol uses a liquid spray delivery format to support efficient absorption of its active compounds.

Liquid Spray Delivery System
Delivery Format

The foundation of the Breathiterol formula is its liquid delivery format. Research published in journals such as the National Institutes of Health's PubMed Central has examined how liquid formulations may offer absorption advantages over solid-dose forms for certain compounds. The oral liquid format bypasses some of the dissolution steps required of capsules and tablets, potentially allowing active ingredients to become bioavailable faster.

This delivery mechanism is central to how Breathiterol positions itself — not just the ingredients themselves, but how those ingredients are delivered to the body.

Respiratory Support Botanical Complex
Core Formula

Breathiterol's core formula targets the respiratory system. Botanical compounds with a history of use in respiratory wellness include herbs such as:

  • Mullein Leaf — Mullein (Verbascum thapsus) has been used in traditional herbal medicine as an expectorant to support airway comfort. The NIH's National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health notes botanical expectorants are widely used for respiratory support.
  • Eucalyptus Extract — Eucalyptol, derived from eucalyptus leaves, has been studied for its potential to support airway clearance and respiratory comfort. Research indicates eucalyptol may support healthy mucus clearance in the respiratory tract.
  • Peppermint Extract — Menthol, found in peppermint, is widely recognized for its cooling, airway-opening sensation and is commonly included in respiratory wellness formulas for comfort support.
  • Ivy Leaf Extract — Ivy leaf (Hedera helix) is included in traditional respiratory preparations and has been studied in European herbal medicine contexts for airway support.

⚠️ Note: These ingredient categories represent common respiratory supplement ingredients based on the formula's stated benefits. Always check the current product label for the exact ingredient list. Contact the manufacturer for the complete supplement facts panel.

Immune Support Nutrient Blend
Wellness Formula

Breathiterol also targets immune health alongside respiratory support. Common immune-supporting nutrients found in respiratory wellness supplements include:

  • Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid) — The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements notes that vitamin C contributes to immune defense by supporting various cellular functions. It is one of the most well-researched immune-supporting nutrients available.
  • Zinc — Zinc plays a role in normal immune system function. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements confirms zinc is essential for immune cell development and function.
  • Elderberry Extract — Black elderberry (Sambucus nigra) is widely studied in immune health research. A 2016 study published in Nutrients examined elderberry's potential role in supporting immune health.

⚠️ These ingredients represent plausible immune-support components based on product category standards. Individual ingredients vary by product formulation batch and region. Verify with the manufacturer's official supplement facts panel.

Antioxidant & Protective Compounds
Protective Formula

Respiratory tissue is exposed to oxidative stress from environmental pollutants, seasonal challenges, and normal metabolic processes. Antioxidant compounds in a lung support formula may help protect respiratory tissue. Common antioxidants in this category include:

  • N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC) — NAC is a precursor to glutathione, the body's primary antioxidant. Research suggests NAC may support mucociliary clearance and respiratory health. It has been studied extensively in the context of lung health support.
  • Quercetin — A natural flavonoid with antioxidant properties, quercetin has been studied for its potential role in supporting respiratory and immune wellness.
  • Vitamin E — Vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements notes it plays roles in immune function and acts as an antioxidant to protect cells from damage.
